# ANDA home: surface brief

Scope: ANDA home page (Persuade) plus site-wide header/footer accents. Audience: neurodivergent people, parents/caregivers, teachers and professionals across Africa, often on phones and slow connections. Job: understand what ANDA is and take a first step (start with NeuRafiki, or pick a pathway). Constraints: brief-pinned palette roles (teal support, terracotta/coral connection, gold/amber attention, indigo assessment, warm neutral ground), Fraunces + Source Sans 3 kept by owner choice, sensory settings (calm/vibrant, motion, contrast, density) must keep working, no stock photos (illustration only, photo spots later), no invented stats.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is a strip-woven cloth. Like kente and aso-oke, narrow woven bands are sewn into one cloth, and the support journey is the thread running through them. It refuses the centred SaaS hero, the fake metric row and the grid of identical icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Warm neutral ground; full-width woven strips (teal, gold, terracotta, indigo, charcoal blocks with weft lines) as the header edge and section seams; bògòlanfini-style linework (dashes, zigzags, dots, nested squares) as a quiet texture; one committed deep-teal band; a solid terracotta closing field. Fraunces display, Source Sans body. Thread-markers, not icon tiles.
STORY: The visitor sees the whole journey as a loop, taps a step to learn what happens and where, believes ANDA and NeuRafiki connect real tools, then starts with NeuRafiki or a pathway.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: headline, supporting line, "Start with NeuraFiki" primary and "Explore ANDA". Right: an interactive six-step journey loop (Assess, Understand, Plan, Support, Measure, Adapt) on a mudcloth-textured panel. A detail card below the ring shows the selected step with its link. A woven strip edges the header.
FORM: Strip-woven textile composition, the owner-chosen evolution of the incumbent world (extension, no concept roll). Seed key: none, an established world.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
