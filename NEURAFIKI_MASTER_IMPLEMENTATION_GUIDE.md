## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Neurafiki** is a movement to transform how Africa understands and supports neurodivergent minds. As a foundation program of Become Change, it combines:
- **Authentic storytelling** (lived experience from Pauline's perspective as mother & systems thinker)
- **Practical support tools** (Parent Circles, Home Toolkit, School Training)
- **Research & policy influence** (locally-grounded knowledge for global impact)
- **Cultural reframing** (replacing shame with dignity, diagnosis with destiny)

This operationalization plan turns the strategic vision into concrete actions, timelines, budgets, and measurable outcomes.

## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

### 1. Authentic Storytelling
- Pauline's voice must remain genuine and grounded
- Stories must reflect real African contexts
- Avoid Western-centric frameworks
- Cultural sensitivity in all content

### 2. Community Building
- Parent circles must feel safe and peer-led
- Facilitate genuine connection
- Measure by engagement, not just numbers
- Continuous feedback and improvement

### 3. Accessibility
- Multi-language support from day 1
- Free or low-cost resources
- Multiple formats (text, video, audio)
- Plain language and clear communication

### 4. Local Partnerships
- Schools, educators, organizations must co-lead
- External expertise combined with local wisdom
- Win-win partnerships
- Long-term commitment

### 5. Sustainable Model
- Don't depend on one funding source
- Build toward earned revenue
- Volunteer/community contributions
- Scale thoughtfully

### 6. Evidence & Impact
- Document everything
- Measure early and often
- Share results transparently
- Adapt based on learning

## POTENTIAL OBSTACLES & MITIGATION

### Obstacle: "Not another African charity"
**Mitigation:** Position as movement and peer support, not charity. Emphasize dignity and agency.

### Obstacle: Language barriers
**Mitigation:** Multilingual from day 1. Hire native speakers. Cultural adaptation not just translation.

### Obstacle: Founder burnout
**Mitigation:** Hire immediately. Build team. Set boundaries. Plan for Pauline's sustainability.

### Obstacle: Low engagement in digital spaces
**Mitigation:** Blend digital and in-person. Use SMS/WhatsApp. Work with organizations with reach.

### Obstacle: Difficult to measure behavior change
**Mitigation:** Track both metrics and stories. Use comparison groups. Longitudinal studies.

### Obstacle: Government gatekeeping
**Mitigation:** Build from bottom-up first. Show demand. Engage early. Use evidence.
