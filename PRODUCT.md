# ANDA: African Neurodiversity Alliance

Part of BecomeChange Institute's two-product ecosystem. ANDA is "Plan. Support. Progress.": support profiles, IEP / support plans, goals, progress, support teams, reports, Find Support, learning, community, advocacy, apps and tools, news and research. Its sister product NeuRafiki ("Assess. Understand. Accompany.") does structured screening and the AI companion. The two stay separate products with separate billing, linked by consent.

## Who it serves
Neurodivergent people, parents and caregivers, teachers, therapists and schools across Africa. Many use phones on slow or costly connections. Many have no specialist nearby and no diagnosis yet.

## What it must do
Help people understand, plan, support, measure, learn and adapt, as one journey with NeuRafiki. ANDA must work on its own, without a NeuRafiki assessment.

## Brand commitments
- Visual world: "African Calm + Intelligent Warmth", rendered as a strip-woven textile (kente and aso-oke strips, bògòlanfini linework) on a warm neutral ground. Teal for support, terracotta for connection, gold for attention, indigo for assessment and NeuRafiki, green for growth.
- Type: Fraunces display with Source Sans 3 body (owner choice, kept).
- Not clinical, not childish, not sterile. Calm technology: inform without overwhelming.
- Imagery: illustration and pattern only until real, consented photos of African families, children and educators are supplied. No stock photos.
- Truth: no invented statistics, testimonials, organisations or compliance claims. Screening is never described as diagnosis.
- Accessibility first: sensory settings (calm/vibrant, motion, text size, contrast, density), reduced motion, keyboard and screen-reader support.
- Product name spelling: "NeuRafiki".
