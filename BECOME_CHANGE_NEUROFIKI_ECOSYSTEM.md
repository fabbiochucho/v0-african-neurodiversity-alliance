## The Integration Points: How They Work Together

### 1. Self-Assessment → Neurafiki Recommendations
```
User completes ANDA self-assessment
├─ Scores communication, sensory, social domains
├─ Identifies strengths and challenges
└─ Triggers automatic Neurafiki recommendations:
   ├─ "Strengthen Communication" → Neurafiki strategy guide
   ├─ "Manage Sensory Needs" → Home-based techniques
   └─ Success stories from similar families
```

### 2. IEP Creation → Neurafiki Context
```
ANDA generates personalized IEP goals
├─ Pulls from 1000+ goal library
├─ Links each goal to Neurafiki resources:
│  ├─ "Why this matters" (narrative)
│  ├─ "How to support this" (strategy)
│  └─ "What families did" (success story)
└─ User creates plan with cultural confidence
```

### 3. Progress Tracking → Neurafiki Learning
```
User logs weekly progress on goals
├─ Standard rating (1-5)
├─ Context capture:
│  ├─ "What helped?"
│  ├─ "What was challenging?"
│  └─ "Who noticed progress?"
├─ Smart suggestions:
│  ├─ If struggling: "Try this Neurafiki strategy"
│  ├─ If progressing: "Try this next phase"
│  └─ Always: "Here's a relevant story"
└─ Data feeds Neurafiki research
```

### 4. Community → Neurafiki Content Creation
```
Community discussions & stories
├─ Parents share experiences
├─ Moderators identify themes
├─ Researchers document patterns
├─ Content team creates new materials
└─ Loop: New content recommended to similar families
```

### 5. Research → Better Platform
```
Neurafiki research on effectiveness
├─ Which strategies show progress?
├─ Which stories resonate with whom?
├─ Which communities need support?
├─ Which IEP goals matter most?
└─ ANDA improves recommendations and features
```

## User Personas & Their Journeys

### Persona 1: Aisha - Parent in Ghana
**Background:** Mother of 6-year-old with communication delays

**ANDA Journey:**
1. Finds ANDA online
2. Takes self-screening (gets clear insights)
3. Creates IEP with 5 communication goals
4. Uses Neurafiki home-based strategies (free, works at home)
5. Logs progress weekly
6. Joins Neurafiki community in Twi (her language)
7. Shares her story (becomes proof point)

**Neurafiki Value:**
- Gets culturally relevant guidance (extended family included)
- No cost for content
- Stories from other Ghanaian families
- Connects to community of parents

**Become Change Impact:**
- Starts informal community group in her neighborhood
- Educates church community about neurodiversity
- Advocates with school for accommodations
- Becomes local facilitator for Neurafiki training

---

### Persona 2: David - Teacher in Kenya
**Background:** Classroom teacher with 3 neurodivergent students, limited training

**ANDA Journey:**
1. School principal enrolls in ANDA institutional plan
2. David accesses educator resources
3. Uses IEP guides to understand student goals
4. Implements Neurafiki school inclusion strategies
5. Tracks student progress in ANDA dashboard
6. Joins Neurafiki educator community

**Neurafiki Value:**

- Practical classroom strategies
- Understanding neurodiversity beyond deficits
- Professional development

**Become Change Impact:**
- Becomes school champion for inclusion

- Shapes school policy on accommodations
- Contributes to Neurafiki school inclusion research

## Why This Matters: The Bigger Picture

### Problem Being Solved
- 15-25% of African population is neurodivergent
- Most lack diagnosis, support, understanding
- Current solutions: Western, expensive, culturally misaligned
- Result: Families isolated, potential untapped, talent wasted

### Solution Being Built
- ANDA: Digital access to screening, planning, tracking
- Neurafiki: Cultural wisdom, family support, systems change
- Together: First comprehensive African neurodiversity ecosystem
- Result: Millions empowered, policies changed, Africa leads globally

### Become Change Connection
- Not charity ("we'll help you")
- Not therapy ("we'll fix you")
- But: "We take responsibility for creating the systems we want to see"
- Become Change through Neurafiki by reshaping neurodiversity narrative
