## CONTENT FORMAT STRATEGY

### Text (40% of content)
- **Long-form essays** (1,500-2,500 words): Deep dives, theory pieces, policy briefs
- **Medium articles** (700-1,000 words): Stories, practical guides, reflections
- **Short pieces** (300-500 words): Updates, insights, quick lessons
- **Downloadable guides** (2-10 pages): Practical tools, templates, workbooks

### Video (30% of content)
- **Story documentaries** (8-12 min): Family stories, parent journeys
- **How-to videos** (5-7 min): Practical demonstrations
- **Interview series** (10-15 min): Expert conversations, parent panels
- **Short clips** (1-2 min): Social media highlights, key insights

### Audio (20% of content)
- **Podcast episodes** (30-45 min): Deep conversations, theory discussions
- **Audio guides** (15-20 min): Meditation, regulation strategies
- **Audiobook versions** (of written content)
- **Voice notes** (from parents/community)

### Interactive (10% of content)
- **Downloadable tools** (assessments, planners, guides)
- **Webinars & live events** (monthly)
- **Discussion prompts** (for communities)
- **Reflective exercises** (for parents)

## DISTRIBUTION STRATEGY

### Primary Channels
1. **ANDA Website** (neurafiki.anda.org)
   - Central hub for all content
   - Organized by pillar, theme, type
   - Searchable and tagged
   
2. **Email Newsletter** (Weekly)
   - Story highlights
   - New resources
   - Community announcements
   - Subscriber: 10,000+ by end of year 1
   
3. **Social Media** (Multiple platforms)
   - Instagram (storytelling focus, 15,000+ followers)
   - LinkedIn (thought leadership, policy reach)
   - Twitter (engagement, conversation)
   - TikTok (reaching younger audiences)
   - Facebook Groups (community building)
   
4. **Podcast Platforms**
   - Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Google Podcasts
   - Target: 5,000+ subscribers by month 6
   
5. **YouTube Channel**
   - Neurafiki-branded channel
   - Target: 10,000+ subscribers by month 12

### Secondary Channels
- Medium (republished long-form content)
- Linkedin Articles (thought leadership)
- Policy platforms (for briefs)
- Academic platforms (for research)
- Partner organization channels
