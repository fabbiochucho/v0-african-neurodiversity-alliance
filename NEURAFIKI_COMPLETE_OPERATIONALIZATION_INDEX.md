**Q: How many parent circles will we have?**

**Q: What's the budget for year 1?**

**Q: Who funds this?**  
A: Diverse sources - foundations (30%), institutional partnerships (40%), individuals (20%), earned revenue (10%).

**Q: Can Pauline do this part-time?**  
A: No. It requires 40 hours/week minimum. A team shares responsibilities.

**Q: When will we see impact?**  
A: Early signs in month 2 (engagement, growth). Measurable impact by month 6 (behavior change, story testimonials).

**Q: Is this scalable?**  
A: Yes. Digital content scales infinitely. Community model scales through facilitators and volunteers.
